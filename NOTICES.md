Live aircraft data is provided by [ADSB.fi](https://adsb.fi/), subject to its own terms. The MIT licence covers this widget's source, not ownership or licensing of feed data, provider names or linked services.

Uses the installed Omarchy and Quickshell APIs. Plugin structure was informed by Omarchy's first-party widgets and Fred Nix's MIT-licensed Pingboard. No Omarchy runtime source is redistributed in the installed plugin or source archive.

Nashville listening hints use public [FAA charts and frequency information](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/flight_info/aeronav/) and [NOAA Memphis Center sector outlines](https://www.weather.gov/zme/zme). [LiveATC](https://www.liveatc.net/) is an external service opened through its public directory pages. The widget does not embed or redistribute audio.
