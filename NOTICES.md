# Notices

Live aircraft data is provided by [ADSB.fi](https://adsb.fi/), subject to its own terms. The MIT licence covers this widget's source, not ownership or licensing of feed data, provider names or linked services.

Uses the installed Omarchy and Quickshell APIs. Plugin structure was informed by Omarchy's first-party widgets and Fred Nix's MIT-licensed Pingboard. No Omarchy runtime source is redistributed in the installed plugin or source archive.

Listening hints use public [FAA charts and frequency information](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/flight_info/aeronav/), [FAA Atlanta runway data](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aip_html/part3_ad_2.0_georgia.html) and [NOAA Memphis Center sector outlines](https://www.weather.gov/zme/zme). The bundled snapshot contains public-use airport records from the [FAA Aviation Facilities dataset](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/aviation-facilities), effective 29 October 2026, and [FAA upper airspace boundaries](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=67885972e4e940b2aa6d74024901c561), retrieved 25 November 2026. [LiveATC](https://www.liveatc.net/) is an external service opened through its public directory pages. The widget does not embed or redistribute audio.
